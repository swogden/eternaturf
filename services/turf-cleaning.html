WHY PROFESSIONAL CLEANING

Turf is low-maintenance, not no-maintenance.

Even the best turf install collects pollen, debris, and pet odor over time — and drainage and infill depth matter as much to how it performs as how it looks. Professional cleaning restores all of it without harsh chemicals.

01
Removes pet odor at the source

Built-up waste and odor get cleared from deep in the turf, not just masked on the surface.

02
Clears pollen and debris

Seasonal pollen and general debris buildup are removed, restoring the clean, green look of a fresh install.

03
Restores drainage performance

Debris and compacted infill can slow drainage over time — cleaning restores the turf's ability to drain the way it did on day one.

04
Checks and restores infill depth

Infill settles and shifts with use — part of a proper cleaning service is restoring it to the right depth for cushioning and fiber support.

05
No harsh chemicals

Cleaning is done in a way that's safe for the kids and pets who use the turf every day.
