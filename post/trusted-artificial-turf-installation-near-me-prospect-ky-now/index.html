Why Prospect Homeowners Are Replacing Natural Grass

Across the country, outdoor living investments continue to rise. In 2025, industry reports showed that landscape improvements remain one of the most requested home upgrades, especially low-maintenance lawn replacements.

In Prospect and nearby communities like Norton Commons, homeowners often face:

Heavy clay soil that holds water
Shaded yards where grass struggles to grow
Pet damage and persistent odors
High maintenance and watering demands

Artificial turf provides a consistent green appearance throughout the year without mowing, fertilizing, or reseeding. It eliminates mud and reduces water usage, making it a practical solution for Kentucky properties.

What Proper Artificial Turf Installation Looks Like

A successful artificial turf install near you depends on what happens beneath the surface.

Professional installation includes removing existing grass and debris, excavating to the proper depth, installing and compacting a crushed stone base, ensuring correct drainage grading, securing seams properly, applying infill, and completing a final brush and inspection.

Base preparation is especially important in Prospect, KY. The local soil composition can shift if not properly compacted, which is why drainage and stabilization are critical steps.

Skipping these steps can lead to uneven surfaces or pooling water. A professionally installed system is designed to remain stable for years.

Does Artificial Turf Require Maintenance?

Artificial turf is low maintenance compared to natural grass.

Basic upkeep includes occasional rinsing, removing leaves or debris, and brushing fibers to maintain appearance. Pet areas may require more frequent rinsing depending on usage.

There is no mowing, fertilizing, aerating, or reseeding required.
